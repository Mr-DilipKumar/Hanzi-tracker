声调 · Pronunciation Lab

Master Mandarin Tones & Tone Pairs

Tones are the melody and meaning of Mandarin. Explore the 5 pitch contours, drill all 20 tone pair combinations, and test your listening ear with interactive quizzes.

The 5 Mandarin Tones

Pitch contour representation on the traditional 5-point scale (5=High, 1=Low).

Rule 1 · 3rd Tone Sandhi
3 + 3 → 2 + 3

When two 3rd tones occur consecutively, the first becomes a 2nd tone. Example: 你好 (nǐ hǎo → ní hǎo).

Rule 2 · 不 (bù) Sandhi
不 + 4 → 2 + 4

不 is 4th tone (bù), but changes to 2nd tone (bú) before another 4th tone. Example: 不是 (bù shì → bú shì).

Rule 3 · 一 (yī) Sandhi
一 + 1/2/3 → 4 | 一 + 4 → 2

一 (yī) becomes 4th tone (yì) before tones 1, 2, 3 (一天, 一年), and 2nd tone (yí) before tone 4 (一定).
